Grilled Salmon with Roasted Vegetables

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4.9) 124 reviews
👥
Servings
4 people
⏱️
Prep Time
15 min
🍳
Cook Time
20 min
📊
Calories
385 kcal

Ingredients

Instructions

1

Preheat your grill to medium-high heat. Season salmon fillets with salt, pepper, and minced garlic.

2

Toss broccoli and bell peppers with olive oil, salt, and pepper. Arrange on a grill pan.

3

Grill vegetables for 10-12 minutes, turning occasionally until tender and lightly charred.

4

Grill salmon for 4-5 minutes per side until cooked through and flakes easily with a fork.

5

Serve immediately with lemon slices and enjoy your healthy, protein-packed meal!
